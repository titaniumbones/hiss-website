## mission

The Humanities and Interpretive Social Sciences Research Hub (HISS) fosters cross-disciplinary community and collaborative research among faculty at the University of Toronto Scarborough. We bring together scholars from diverse fields to explore complex questions about human experience, culture, and society.

Through innovative research, interdisciplinary dialogue, and community building, we advance understanding of the humanities and social sciences while addressing contemporary challenges facing our world.

## vision

> We envision a vibrant intellectual community where scholars from diverse disciplines collaborate to generate new knowledge, challenge conventional thinking, and contribute to positive social change.
