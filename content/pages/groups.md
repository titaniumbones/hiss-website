## intro

Each group brings together four to six faculty from at least two departments, across ranks and across the teaching and research streams. At the first meeting each group drafts its own working agreement: what each member brings, what each hopes to get out of it, and how the group will share facilitation, workload and credit. Groups then meet through the winter term, with food provided.

The research groups are supported by the University of Toronto Mentoring Partnership Fund. Three of the proposed research groups will run this year, chosen by the number of colleagues who sign up for each.

## group-critical-ai

### Critical AI

What generative AI is doing to reading, writing, teaching and scholarship, approached from the humanities and interpretive social sciences rather than from engineering. The group will open with a public lecture in the winter term and close with a workshop at the end of term.

## group-democratic-futures

### Democratic Futures

Humanistic approaches to reconceiving democracy: who draws the lines and who gets to deliberate, from electoral redistricting to citizens' assemblies, participatory budgeting, Indigenous governance, and the democracies imagined in literature and art.

## group-evaluation-crisis

### The Evaluation Crisis

Ungrading, contract grading and other alternatives to conventional assessment, at a moment when generative AI has unsettled the take-home essay. A working group for colleagues who want to try something different in their own courses and compare notes.

## group-migration-diaspora

### Migration, Diaspora, and Belonging

Migration, displacement, diaspora communities, citizenship and belonging, studied through literature, film, ethnography, geography, history and language. Many of our colleagues work on these questions, and Scarborough is one of the places they study.

## group-embodied-practice

### Embodied Practice

No agenda beyond getting together and making things. This group is open to everyone and doesn't need a sign-up: it starts with an evening of board games in December, with details to follow on the calendar.
