## intro

The Humanities and Interpretive Social Sciences Research Hub (HISS) is a vibrant intellectual community bringing together faculty from diverse disciplines to explore complex questions about human experience, culture, and society.

Through collaborative research, interdisciplinary dialogue, and innovative scholarship, we advance understanding of the humanities and social sciences while addressing contemporary challenges facing our world.
