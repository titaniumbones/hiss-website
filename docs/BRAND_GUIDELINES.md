# University of Toronto Brand Guidelines

Research compiled from official UofT Brand Portal and supporting sources.

## Official Colors

- Hex: `#1E3765`
- RGB: `30, 55, 101`
- CMYK: `100, 79, 12, 59`

### Secondary Colors

**Accent Blue/Teal (Pantone 633)**

- Hex: `#007FA3`
- RGB: `0, 127, 163`

**Dark Gray**

- Hex: `#333333`
- RGB: `51, 51, 51`

**Medium Gray**

- Hex: `#555555`

**Error Red**

- Hex: `#E3140D`

## Typography

### Primary Typeface: Trade Gothic Next

- Main institutional typeface
- Web fallbacks: Space Grotesk, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif
- When unavailable: Arial Narrow or Helvetica Medium Condensed

### Secondary Typeface: Bembo

- Use sparingly for body text only
- Fallback: Times New Roman, Georgia, serif

## UTSC Specific Branding

### Visual Identity

UTSC has a colorful graphic identity where the multiplicity of colors reflects UTSC's diversity of thought, discipline, heritage, and culture. The colors are symbolic of the Highland Creek setting.

**Tagline**: "Tomorrow is created here"

### Logo Requirements

- University of Toronto principal marks must appear in solid color
- UTSC logo should be obtained from official brand portal
- Ensure proper clearspace and sizing per brand guidelines

## Accessibility Requirements

### WCAG 2.1 Level AA Compliance

- **Normal text**: Minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1
- **Large text** (18pt+ or 14pt+ bold): Minimum contrast ratio of 3:1
- **Interactive elements**: Minimum contrast ratio of 3:1

### Color Contrast Verification

- U of T Blue (#1E3765) on white: ~9.8:1 ✓ (Excellent)
- Accent Blue (#007FA3) on white: ~4.6:1 ✓ (AA compliant)
- Dark Gray (#333333) on white: ~12.6:1 ✓ (Excellent)

## Logo Usage

- Logo files available at: [U of T Brand Portal](https://brand.utoronto.ca/)
- Must maintain proper clearspace
- No distortion or modification
- Use official color versions only
